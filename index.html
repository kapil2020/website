What the work found

Four numbers from my dissertation and the DRUM routing engine. Each one is a published result, not a rounded claim.

LEAP route
−0%

exposure in Central Delhi when commuters take the least-exposure route, at a cost of +40% travel time.

LECR route
−0%

How the work runs
01

Measure

Stated-preference panels and on-road sensing across transport micro-environments.

02

Model

Hybrid latent-class and ICLV discrete choice models of who changes, and who cannot.

03

Learn

Deep and geostatistical models filling the gaps between sparse monitoring stations.

04

Ship

DRUM: a live routing engine returning the least-exposure and least-energy route.

05

Inform

Evidence for transport policy, TOD prioritisation and active-mobility investment.
