\section{Profile}

{\fontsize{9.4}{13}\selectfont\color{body}%
I am a transportation researcher working on how people decide to travel --- which mode, which
route, at which hour --- and how those decisions meet the environment they move through. My
attention is on the moment behaviour \emph{changes}: under air pollution, under heat, under a new
piece of infrastructure or a new piece of information. I design stated-preference and panel
experiments, estimate hybrid latent class, ICLV and mixed logit models, develop new estimation
methods, and build the software the work runs on. The apparatus belongs to behavioural economics
and decision analytics as much as to transportation engineering.\par}
